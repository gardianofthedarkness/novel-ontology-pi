Read any novel on this Mac

Add a folder: every EPUB and text file in it, and in the folders inside it, is found. Your files are never moved or changed; what the console learns is kept in a hidden .novel folder beside them.

  1. Add a folder of books.
  2. Keys: Jev's and the work model's. They stay in this Mac's secure store.
  3. Press ▶ beside a book to read it (about $0.35 a volume). It runs on its own; you can close the window.
  4. Open the book. The icons on the right open its characters, places and strands, the reading's details for a line, and your agents' sessions.
