From a novel to a world you can watch

Extraction reads the book into a world model: who says and does each line, when it happens, where every place and thing is. The world engine builds that world on the ground: the terrain first, then where everyone stands, then how they move.

  • The program does geometry; Jev does meaning. No model is asked for a coordinate or a size.
  • Jev sees what the user sees: every terrain question carries pictures of the world as it stands.
  • One context engine composes every Jev request: the raw text around the place's name, ranked by the question, nothing padded.
  • Pins are suggestions; placement is one optimization, settled part by part.
