EM across the novel — the strict backstop

status: implemented repo: src/solidifier/matching/ (structuralMatch, emFaculty, entityMatch) CLI: novel match · novel fix merge

Birth resolution (08a) catches most duplicates. What it misses — spelling twins, under-threshold declines, referents named only in later chapters — is the EM faculty's job. It re-checks identity structurally, across the whole graph, and it is deliberately strict: a miss is cheap, because the check re-runs every block and scores only grow — while a bad merge is permanent.

Design details

  • Structural nomination, strict bar: score ≥ 0.6, with a support floor of ≥ 2 shared scenes or ≥ 3 shared neighbors. Zero co-actor events is a veto — two figures who act together are not the same person. Alias-linked pairs are excluded outright.
  • One batched checklist per pass: nominated pairs go through a single batched Jev checklist, not one call per pair. The checklist is the only model step; nomination itself is code.
  • Two rhythms: per block, only pairs touching new entities are checked. At chapter close, the old × old sweep runs over every pair the block passes skipped.
  • Distinct verdicts are remembered: a pair judged distinct is written to matching.json and skipped until its shared evidence grows by +2. Scores only grow, so a miss today can become a merge next chapter — safely.
  • Merges never bypass the audit: a confirmed merge goes through the audited post-apply mergeEntities path, never as a gate proposal. EM composes under the semantic fact gate (decision #17): the revision digest fails closed on pending reviews.

Implementation

  • src/solidifier/matching/structuralMatch.ts — buildStructuralIndex / nominateStructuralCandidates: the nomination pass over shared scenes and neighbors.
  • src/solidifier/matching/emFaculty.ts — the EM faculty: per-block passes over new-entity pairs, the chapter-close old × old sweep, and the matching.json memory (read/write, +2 growth rule).
  • src/solidifier/matching/entityMatch.ts — thresholds: CONFIDENT_THRESHOLD = 0.85, UNCERTAIN_THRESHOLD = 0.45, DISTINCT_THRESHOLD = 0.6; nominateMatchCandidates for the novel match CLI surface.
  • src/solidifier/maintenance/fixes.ts — mergeEntities: the audited merge path. Follow-up open: novel fix merge does not emit join events yet.
  • Matched entities get a playground join animation (orbital morph + classic glide) — the user sees the merge happen in the graph (sector 13).
