# Pick test: how Jev picks a building on a map (2026-11-03)

The user's question ([Decisions](../v3/milestones.html#director-decisions) 11): does Jev pick more precisely with vectors drawn in the picture, with
semantic descriptions, or with small thumbnail references? `experiments/v2/pick-test.mjs`: cases with a known answer,
computed from the world's geometry, at three spots each of scenes 1 and 26 (six candidate buildings a spot, apart in
height and place); three questions, each asked four ways on the same top-down map. 64 Jev requests (jev-1.14.0), then 16 more for the two turns.
Back to [Directing](../v3/design.html#directing-notes).

| Way | Right | Tallest | Nearest | On the way to the tallest | Confidence right / wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain map (lettered spheres, you marked) | 9/16 (56%) | 1/6 | 5/6 | 3/4 | 0.55 / 0.28 |
| Vectors (an arrow from you to each) | 10/16 (63%) | 2/6 | 5/6 | 3/4 | 0.62 / 0.34 |
| Semantic (each letter in words: floors, how far, which way on the map) | 15/16 (94%) | 6/6 | 6/6 | 3/4 | 0.83 / 0.33 |
| Thumbnails (a close-up of each, marked) | 8/16 (50%) | 1/6 | 5/6 | 2/4 | 0.56 / 0.30 |
| Side view: v1's turn 2 alone (the lettered buildings from the side at two turns, almost level, a pole as a ruler) | 12/16 (75%) | 6/6 | 5/6 | 1/4 | |
| **Two turns** (v1's height lock: the map's distribution times the side view's) | 13/16 (81%) | 6/6 | 5/6 | 2/4 | |
| Two turns, each weighed by Jev's sureness in it | 13/16 (81%) | 5/6 | 6/6 | 2/4 | |

What it says, with few cases a cell:

- **Height cannot be read from a top-down map**, and the thumbnails did not help (1/6). Words carry it (6/6).
- **Nearness is read well from the picture** (5/6 any way).
- **The spatial question** (which building lies on the way to the tallest) scores alike every way (3/4); the one miss
  was the same building every time, which may be the answer key's own ambiguity (a building near you, just off the line).
- **Jev's confidence is calibrated**: higher when right than wrong every way, most clearly with words (0.83 against
  0.33), so its answers can be gated on it.
- Drawn vectors add a little (63% against 56%); thumbnails as drawn (a far view for a very tall tower, a crowded one for
  a building among others) did not.

**v1's two-turn height lock** (the user: "you should be able to find the two turn height locking design in v1.
Quantum applied to boxes"; `world-engine/view/two-view-things.mjs`, docs/v3/design.html#stage-simulation-design: one view is weak on
height, a second at right angles fixes it; find by a choice, then lock): turn 2 shows the same lettered buildings from
the side, and the two turns' whole distributions multiply, each a measurement in its own basis (the map where, the side
view how high). With pictures alone it takes height from 1/6 to 6/6, and the whole from 56% to 81% (16 more requests,
the map's from the cache). Words still read best (94%), and the plain product loses one spatial case the map alone got
(the side view, a measurement in the wrong basis for where, adds noise); weighing each turn by Jev's sureness in it
scores the same with these few cases.

Done since: the landmark question (the mega-scene director) describes each building in words with its floors, distance
and which way on the map it lies, and asks v1's second turn from the side; the two turns' distributions multiply.
