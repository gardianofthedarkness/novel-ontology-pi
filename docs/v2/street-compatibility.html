Street × street: may they adjoin?

Compatible kinds may touch, not lie over each other: a box mostly over a compatible kept place is asked again, and compatible parts still overlapping are moved beside each other (the pool expanding, nothing vanishing). Before Jev boxes a pool's member, its suggested spot moves onto free ground; a box over an incompatible place is asked again. Either side may name the other. Anything kept below the street lies under anything. Across levels the tree decides: a street may not lie over a district that does not hold it. Source: OVERLAPS, mayOverlap in src/v2/context/terrainModes.ts; CROWDED, nearestFree in src/v2/context/zoom.ts.

Street kind × building use

Each street kind's form lays out its own buildings; each row building takes a use from its kind's list (the share shown), and a form's pieces have their own (a centre's mall podium and office towers, a station's hall). A building tied to a street by continuity dwells in one of its buildings of its use, Jev not asked; a use no street has (flagged) is always planted by Jev. Source: uses in LANDSCAPES, src/ground/stage/landscape.ts.

Underground × street: what lies under what

The city's common underground spaces, grouped as the literature does: transport (the subway's tunnels, stations and passages), commerce (an underground shopping street, under a road or under a station's plaza, as Japan's 地下街), parking, utilities (a utility tunnel, the storm water), a civil defence shelter (often a car park in peace), a building's basement, and the deep facilities. Each usually lies under the street kinds marked; anything kept below the street may share ground with anything above it. A part kept below the street becomes the hall its name says (the words below), else a passage or a hall by its proportions. Source: LIES_UNDER in src/ground/stage/underground.ts.
