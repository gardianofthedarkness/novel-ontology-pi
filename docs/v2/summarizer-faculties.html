Summarizer Faculties

A plan for the director's readers and directors as measuring instruments: a cheap coarse reading of every block says which observables are active where, and only there do the fine faculties read and the specialist directors ask.

ProposalTrack A · A4–A53 Nov 2026Volume 1 measured

Why

The summarizer run (d20261103060830) played far better than any before it: 743 moves against W8's 64, on 269 Jev calls instead of 792. Three problems remain, all from one gap: nothing decides a scene's frame, so every card plays at one fixed scale with people left wherever they were.

The camera shifts back and forth

A scene's people end up in groups far apart, and each card frames its own actors. In scene 19, "indoors" moved Misaka 72 m to a basement under an office while the cast stayed at the spawn point. In scene 40, Shokuhou came in from scene 1's roof, 460 m away. There are 31 camera jumps of more than 40 m.

The crowd plays as people

Each walker has its own path, so the crowd reads as characters. It should be a cloud of particles that belongs to the place.

Scale is capped

Jev picks a direction on a picture 16 m across, a leap reaches 30 m, and spots are offered within 100 m. Scene 26 goes from one tower's 44th floor to the next one's 38th, then to a third one's 52nd, across an avenue above a river.

When jev is classifying sentence types, it will output the distribution of all types… we record the top candidates and their distribution all… a corresponding faculty is only activated once it's related types become abnormally dense, signaling it should kick in.The user, 3 Nov 2026

The idea

One pass of Jev already reads every sentence. It gives each sentence a kind, and with this plan each block (about 12 sentences) a type, both as full distributions. The gate turns those distributions into firing spans. Faculties read only their spans and write claims, each with its line and how sure it is, to a scene board the director and smoother read.

Specialist directors

Jev can be asked for different questions based on what the input director sees… we can split our directors into specialist… This is a general mini harness design change, we need to have a pre written and derived from code active summary faculty to director group relationship.The user, 3 Nov 2026

The block types do more than gate the readers: they decide which director is on set. A routine director works inside a room at 2–15 m, asking Jev about formations, who is addressed and how people feel. A mega-scene director works from far away, asking about landmarks, routes and the extent of a catastrophe. A combat director asks the kind and shape of each attack. A transport specialist handles vehicles and weapons, and an architect turns a borrowed building into the one the text describes. One registry in code (world-engine/director/specialists.mjs) holds the whole relationship, and the sections below are written from it.

State and the ontology

Each director keeps a state as it plays. None of it is written into the ontology directly: it goes through the world log, the laws, the smoother and the ledger, and a gate decides whether canon writes it, the review sees it first, or it stays with the run.

  1. playthe director changes its state card by card (positions, exchanges, feelings, holdings, buildings)world-engine/director/director.mjs
  2. world logeach change a record, append-only, with its rank (pinned, stated, met, answered, ruled) and sourceworld-engine/log/world-log.mjs
  3. lawschecked as it plays (held, amended) and over the whole run afterwardsworld-engine/laws/laws.mjs
  4. smootherpositions and presence back-propagated: the least-action history and its clouds (later: feelings, as additive states)world-engine/smoother/
  5. ledgerthe run's proposals to the ontology, each with its evidence and the laws' verdictsworld-engine/log/write-log.mjs
  6. gateapplied by canon where a law checks it, held for the review (Pi) otherwise; some state stays with the runworld-engine/review/canon.mjs, review.mjs
  7. canonwritten into ontology/*.jsonl (last write wins by id), every write logged in canon.jsonl and undoable; the graph rebuiltworld-engine/review/canon.mjs
  8. next runthe review's answers become pins; canon, pins and the smoothing are where the next run starts fromworld-engine/review/review.mjs
applied written by canon without review: a law checks it and nothing broke
held held for the review first; written once answered (some move to applied once audits show they are right)
run kept with the run only (its records, the page); never in the ontology

Firing conditions

A faculty fires on the expected density of its types, read from the distributions rather than the top answer, against the book's own baseline.

density(f, block) = Σ over f's types P(type | block) + Σ over f's signals count ÷ the most in any block baseline(f) = mean μ and spread σ of density(f) over the book f fires on when density ≥ μ + k_on · σ (k_on ≈ 1.5) off when it falls below μ + k_off · σ (k_off ≈ 0.5) and stays on at least two blocks (12 sentences), reading a block of margin either side lead(beat) = the director whose fired faculties weigh most (its own 2, others it reads 1); the rest co-direct
  • Hysteresis: the off line sits below the on line, so a faculty doesn't flicker on a single line.
  • Co-firing: several faculties may be on at once (a fight during a chase fires choreography and journeys).
  • Smoothing: a hidden Markov model over the block types, with Jev's distributions as soft observations, as occupation.mjs does for presence. Its change points are the beats, where the camera may cut.
  • Damping wins over drift: while interior flow, exposition or memory is dense, no implied move or background drift plays.
  • Signals from the reading: named vehicles and weapons fire props (and so the transport specialist), named or described buildings fire the set description (the architect), crowd slots fire ambience, and a jump in the scene frame's scale fires journeys.
  • Who leads: a fight alone is the combat director's; a fight during a chase is the mega-scene director's, with the combat director answering the attack questions; with nothing fired, the routine director leads.
